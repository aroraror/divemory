DiveMory Privacy Policy

Effective: November 25, 2026 · Operator: Araora · Contact: aroraror@naver.com · 한국어

Araora (the "operator") runs the DiveMory app (the "app") and handles your personal information with care, in line with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it is stored and deleted. If this English version and the Korean version differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. Using the app without signing in

The guide, practice quizzes, My log and "Seen it" records work without signing in. When you are not signed in, your study history is stored only on your device and is not sent to any server (if you sign in, the "study history backup" in section 2 is also used). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. The operator does not receive this information.

1-1. In-app purchases

Paid region packs and the all-regions lifetime pass are sold through Google Play billing. Payments and card details are handled by Google; the operator never receives them. The app stores only the purchased product IDs and the time they were verified on your device, so purchased regions open even without internet. This is not sent to the operator's server. You can check purchases and request refunds in your Google Play account. Photos for purchased regions are downloaded through Google Play.

2. Information collected when you sign in, and why

Friends, ranked matches, invite codes, study history backup and content error reports require signing in with Kakao or Google (optional). Find Kakao friends is available only when you sign in with Kakao. When you sign in, the following is stored on the server.

Leaderboards and friend lists show the nickname you set in the app, not your Kakao or Google name.

2-1. Invite codes

When you tap "Create invite code", the server makes a random code and shows it to you. The code contains no personal information, and the server stores only a converted value used for checking, not the code itself. When a friend enters your code, a friend request from you is sent to them, and you become friends only after they accept. Codes expire after 7 days.

3. KakaoTalk friend list (optional, Kakao accounts only)

  • Only if you tap "Find Kakao friends" and agree to Kakao providing your friend list, the nicknames and profile pictures of KakaoTalk friends who use DiveMory are shown on your device screen. This list is not stored on the server or on your device.
  • If you pick a friend from the list and tap "Friend request", that friend's Kakao member number is sent to the server once to find their DiveMory account and create a friend request. The member number is used only to create the request and is not stored.
  • Using "Find Kakao friends" also turns on "Let Kakao friends find me", so your KakaoTalk friends can send you friend requests too (explained on the search screen in advance; you can turn it off any time in Friends settings).
  • You become friends when the other person accepts, or when you both send requests; before that, neither of you can see the other's score or rank. No request is created if the other person has turned off "Let Kakao friends find me" or has blocked you.
  • You can withdraw consent any time in your Kakao account settings. You can accept friend requests you receive even without agreeing to share your friend list.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Sign-in information is kept until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, your account identifiers, profile, friend relationships, invites, blocks, ranked match records, scores, consent records, abuse prevention records and study history backup are deleted immediately, and the connection with Kakao or Google is removed. Study history stored on your device is not deleted (it is removed when you uninstall the app).
  • Content error reports are kept to fix the guide, but only the content is kept; information identifying the sender is deleted.
  • There is currently no information the operator must keep separately by law. Payment and transaction records for in-app purchases are kept by Google; the operator does not receive them (section 1-1).
  • See the account and data deletion guide for how to delete your account.

5. Sharing with third parties

The operator does not provide or sell personal information to other companies or people and does not use it for advertising. Other users see only what you allow: your nickname and profile picture, and your score if you join rankings, shown to your friends. Requests required by law are an exception.

7. How we protect your information

  • All communication between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Every server table has access restrictions (row-level security), so no one can read or change another person's information directly from the app. Scores are calculated only by the server.
  • Administrative secret keys are never put in the app and are accessible only to the operator.

8. Your rights

  • You can change your nickname and profile picture in the app at any time.
  • Joining rankings is off at first. "Let Kakao friends find me" is off at first and turns on when you use Find Kakao friends. You can turn either on or off at any time.
  • You can sign out and delete your account directly in the app.
  • For any other request to access, correct, delete or stop processing your information, contact aroraror@naver.com. We will handle it without delay (within 10 days at the latest) after confirming your identity.

9. Children

Features that need sign-in (friends, ranked matches, study history backup and reports) are for users aged 14 and over. The operator does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 and deletes such accounts as soon as they are found. Anyone can use the guide and practice quizzes without signing in.

10. Information collected automatically

The app does not read advertising IDs, location, contacts, photos or files, and uses no separate analytics or advertising tools. While running the server, access records (access time, IP address, etc.) may remain briefly in the server provider's security logs.

11. Privacy officer and contact

Privacy officer: Araora (operator) · Email aroraror@naver.com

For privacy complaints in Korea, you may also contact the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (118, privacy.kisa.or.kr) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (1833-6972, www.kopico.go.kr).

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page and in the app 7 days before it takes effect.
